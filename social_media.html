Social Media

Social media are websites and apps that let people create a free account, share things like photos, videos, and short messages, and see what other people share. Think of it as a giant online bulletin board where friends, family, and businesses can post updates and talk to each other. For a new company, social media is one of the easiest and least expensive ways to let people know the business exists and to build a relationship with customers.

Social Media Comparison

Facebook
Facebook is the largest social network in the world, used by people of nearly every age group. Businesses can create a free Facebook Page to post news, photos, events, and special offers, answer customer questions through Messenger, and run low-cost ads targeted by location and interests. It is a strong choice for building a local community around a new company.

Source: Facebook_f_logo_(2019).svg
Instagram
Instagram is a photo and video sharing app that is especially popular with younger adults. Companies use it to show off products with eye-catching pictures, short videos called Reels, and 24-hour Stories. Hashtags and partnerships with influencers help a new brand get discovered by people who do not follow it yet.

Source: Instagram_logo_2016.svg
LinkedIn
LinkedIn is a professional networking site where people list their work experience and connect with coworkers and employers. A company page on LinkedIn helps a new business share company news, post job openings, and reach other businesses, which makes it the best fit for business-to-business marketing and building a professional reputation.
